# Spatial Computing & 3D Generative AI Glossary

- **Vertex (v):** A point in 3D Euclidean space defined by $(x, y, z)$ coordinates.
- **Face (f):** A polygon (usually a triangle or quad) formed by connecting vertices.
- **Normals (vn):** Vectors perpendicular to surfaces, determining light reflection and shading.
- **UV Coordinates (vt):** 2D mapping coordinates $(u, v)$ projecting flat textures onto 3D curved surfaces.
- **VAD (Voice Activity Detection):** Neural technique discriminating human speech from background acoustic noise.
- **TTFT (Time to First Token):** Latency from the receipt of prompt to emission of the first response token.
